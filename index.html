the fine print

The boring-but-important bits, tucked away until you need them.

booking and payment
  • A 50% non-refundable deposit secures your date.
  • The final balance is due the day before your event.
  • Events booked within 3 days are paid in full at booking.
delivery and setup
  • Delivery windows are coordinated in advance.
  • Setup usually takes about 30 minutes, depending on the package.
  • You provide the table space and access to the event area.
  • The serving tray and display pieces are included, and yours to keep.
food and freshness
  • Displays are made to be enjoyed right away.
  • For food safety, nothing should sit unrefrigerated for more than 2 hours.
  • Ingredients may be swapped based on what's in season.
